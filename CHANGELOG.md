# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-07

First public version.

- Drag-and-drop TV designer (24 × 12 grid) with settings for every widget.
- 18 widgets, 10 of them new: text, picture, video loop, photo slideshow, digital clock, countdown,
  QR code, scrolling announcements, weather, web page.
- Background photo or video per TV, with a darkness setting.
- One design for every TV, or one per TV; copy a design between TVs.
- Full-screen announcements from reception with movement, colour or picture/video, an optional
  end time, and buttons reception can save themselves.
- The TV keeps working whatever widgets are left off.
- Reception desk from the original project: TV control, uploads with approval, playlists,
  occasions, visitors registry, enquiries inbox, logs.
