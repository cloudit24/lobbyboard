# For reception staff

Log in at `/agent/` and open **TVs**, then the TV you want.

## Put an announcement on the whole screen

The first block on the page.

1. Tap a ready button (**On a break**, **Back in 15 min**, **Prayer**, **Closed**) or one of your
   own (⭐), or type your own text.
2. Optional: a small line under it, a movement (rise, fade, pulse, zoom, slide across), a colour,
   or a picture/video behind it.
3. Optional: **Take it off by itself after** 5 minutes to 1 hour.
4. **Show it now**. The TV changes within a few seconds.

To end it, press **Take it off**.

### Your own buttons

Fill in the announcement the way you like it, open **⭐ Save as my own button**, give it a short
name (e.g. *Lunch break*) and press **Save button**. Everyone at reception sees it. Remove a button
with the × next to it.

## Play something

- **Welcome screen**, **photos** (a list or all approved photos), **a video or a video list**, with or
  without sound, **next video**.
- **Music** under photos: pick a song or list, next song, stop, volume.
- **Occasions**: start or stop a celebration the admin has set up.
- If the TV looks stuck: **Reload the screen**.

## Add your own files

**Upload files**: MP4 videos, MP3/M4A music, JPG/PNG photos. An admin approves them before they can
be played.
